Optical fiber · indoor movement · machine learning

Seeing movement through an optical fiber.

Footsteps create floor vibrations. Distributed acoustic sensing (DAS) measures how those vibrations change along a fiber, turning many sensing locations into a picture of movement.

03 / From vibration to occupant tracking

Learning patterns across time and space.

The study proposes a machine-learning method for footstep recognition using the spatial continuity and temporal dependence of vibration signals. Geophones serve as the benchmark for evaluating DAS. The comparison also examines sensing range, walking modes and walking velocity.
