The study examines how building morphology relates to occupants’ movement. Movement Atlas illustrates this question at two scales. The campus view shows gathering, exchange and dispersal between districts. The building view follows circulation between the seven floors of EA in a model reconstructed from architectural plans. All animated flows are simulated.
